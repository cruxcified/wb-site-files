About Me

I’m Will, a solo game dev from Portland, Oregon. While primarily being a writer, I’ve always drifted toward more visual mediums like film and video games. In 2022, I created my first game, a pixel narrative called Dog Days based off my MFA thesis. Since then, I’ve been ferociously creating in every medium I can get my hands on.

Horror video games have been a part of my life since I was a child growing up in the early 2000s. I spent hours playing games like Silent Hill 4: The Room, Resident Evil 4 and Cry of Fear, and that love for complex narratives exploring the human condition, how people face past traumas and how those traumas haunt them, followed me naturally into my creative writing. Now that I’ve completed my debut novel, SHARDS, I’ve turned my creative eye again toward the visual medium that brought me so much comfort growing up, and I hope to bring my stories to life in a way that evokes those same feelings I had as a child.

What’s on the Tapes

A first-person horror engine and editor, built on three.js. It ships as a single-file editor and a Windows installer.

  1. 01

    Render It Like a PS1

    320×240, vertex snapping, affine textures, 15-bit dithered colour, fog, baked lighting and a flashlight. ▶ Watch it on CH 01

  2. 02

    Paint the Map

    Lay out the level on a grid. The engine turns it into a space you can walk. ▶ Watch it on CH 02

  3. 03

    Write the Dialogue

    Plain text fields, NPCs with animated models, and choices that branch. ▶ Watch it on CH 03

  4. 04

    Direct the Scenes

    A Director mode for cutscenes, plus scripting and plugins for everything else. ▶ Watch it on CH 04

  5. 05

    Build From Inside the Game

    Press B and a hotbar of pieces comes up: floors, walls, doorways, windows, doors and props. A see-through ghost shows where each one goes. ▶ Watch it on CH 05

  6. 06

    Cut the Scenes

    A Cutscene Editor with a timeline for picture, action, props, titles, subtitles, voice, music, sound and blood. Every track has a ＋ that adds at the playhead. ▶ Watch it on CH 06

  7. 07

    Film It in Movie Mode

    Fly the camera, switch between wide, medium and close lenses, set keyframes and record the shot, with VHS tape and cinema bars if you want them. ▶ Watch it on CH 07

  8. 08

    Flip to the Otherworld

    Any room can get a rust-red twin. A story action shudders the room dark and leaves you on the same spot in the other version. ▶ Watch it on CH 08
